# Terms and parent consent

Version 2026-11-04. You read and agree to these when you register. If they change, we will ask you to
agree again.

## What this is

Bar Model Tutor is a **prototype** made for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It is not a finished
product and is not offered commercially. It may change, stop working or be switched off at any time.

## Who may register

- You must be a **parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over**. The account is yours.
- Children use the tutor only through your account (their hero name and the password you set), **with
  your supervision**. You are responsible for how your child uses it.

## How the AI is used

The tutor uses AI models made by **Meta**, through Meta's model API:
- **Muse Spark** reads photos of questions, plans the bar model, double-checks the answer, and replies to
  your child's questions during a lesson.
- **Meta speech recognition** turns your child's voice into text while the microphone is on.
- A version of **Muse Glimmer** that we fine-tuned on maths questions can also plan lessons. It runs on
  **Fireworks AI**, which hosts it for us. It is currently switched off.

## What is sent to these services

To run a lesson, these are sent to Meta, and the question text also to Fireworks AI when Glimmer is on:
- the question your child types, or the photo or PDF they upload;
- your child's **voice** while the microphone is on (the teacher's voice is never sent), and anything they
  type in the lesson.

Meta's and Fireworks AI's own terms and privacy policies apply to what they receive. We do not send them
your name, email, your child's name or hero name, or the progress we keep.

## What we keep

See [privacy.md](privacy.md) (shown as "What we store"). In short: your display name, each child's first name or
nickname, level, avatar, hero name and a hashed password, and their lesson progress and saved lessons. We
never keep photos, audio, transcripts or your email address.

To improve the tutor we also keep the text of questions it **couldn't do**, and anything you send with
**"Report a problem"** (the question, the tutor's plan and your note). The person who runs Bar Model Tutor can
see these, and usage per family (your display name, your children's nicknames, levels and progress) on an
admin page, only to run and improve the service. You can delete a child, or your whole account
and everything in it, at any time from the family panel.

## AI can make mistakes

Every number the tutor shows is checked by code against a verified bar model, and two models must agree on
an answer before it is shown. Even so, the tutor can be wrong, misread a photo or mishear your child. It is
a learning aid, not a replacement for a teacher. If something looks wrong, trust your judgement.

## Fair use

New problems are limited per family per day so the prototype stays available for everyone. Please don't
upload anything that isn't a maths question, or anything personal about your child.

## What you agree to

By ticking the boxes when you register, you confirm that:
1. you are the child's parent or legal guardian and are 18 or over;
2. you agree that your child's questions, photos, voice (while the microphone is on) and typed answers are
   sent to Meta's AI services, and question text to Fireworks AI, to run the lessons, as described above;
3. you will supervise your child's use, and you understand the AI can make mistakes.
